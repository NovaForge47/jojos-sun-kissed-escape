Brand & Design Direction
Palette: mostly warm beige and white as the dominant tones, with black for typography/contrast and a soft ocean blue used sparingly as an accent (buttons, hover states, small wave/water motifs) — not a dominant color.
I'm attaching the logo (circular rose-gold/champagne badge with a cursive "JoJo" monogram, black "JOJO PORTO RAFTI" wordmark, cursive beige "beach bar" script). Pull the warm beige/champagne tone from the logo's circular badge as a secondary accent alongside the ocean blue.
Mood: relaxed, elegant, sun-drenched Mediterranean beach bar — not loud or neon. Generous white space, soft shadows, rounded corners, high-quality beach/lifestyle imagery placeholders.
Typography: a clean modern sans-serif for body text, paired with an elegant serif or script accent (echoing the logo's cursive "JoJo") for headings/section titles.
Fully responsive — most visitors will be on mobile, checking hours or getting directions from the beach.
